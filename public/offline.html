🗣️ Essential Japanese SOS Phrases

たすけてください！
Tasukete kudasai!
Please help me!
津波避難ビルはどこですか？
Tsunami hinan biru wa doko desu ka?
Where is the tsunami evacuation building?
ケガをしています。救急車を呼んでください。
Kega o shite imasu. Kyuukyuusha o yonde kudasai.
I am injured. Please call an ambulance.
外国語（英語）が話せる人はいますか？
Gaikokugo (Eigo) ga hanaseru hito wa imasu ka?
Is there anyone who can speak English?

🏔️ Immediate Survival Rules

Tsunami: Run immediately to ground higher than 15 meters or 4th floor+ of reinforced concrete building. Do NOT wait for official siren.

Earthquake: Drop, Cover, Hold On. Protect your head. Turn off gas stove ONLY after shaking stops.

Typhoon: Stay indoors away from windows. Avoid rivers, underpasses, and coastal roads.
